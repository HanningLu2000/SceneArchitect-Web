SceneArchitect

Progressive 3D Scene Generation with Structural Consolidation and Semantic Grounding

Hanning Lu   ·   Yifan Zhang   ·   Bin Song *
State Key Laboratory of Integrated Services Networks, Xidian University
* Corresponding author.

SceneArchitect. Starting from a lightweight text or image input, the scene is progressively expanded while Structural Consolidation stabilizes geometry and Semantic Grounding preserves scene-level semantics. The accumulated representation is further optimized by depth-regularized staged Gaussian refinement.

Abstract

Progressive 3D scene generation expands an initial view through iterative synthesis and scene integration, enabling scalable scene creation from lightweight inputs. However, errors introduced during expansion can propagate across subsequent views, causing geometric distortion and semantic drift that progressively degrade scene quality. We present SceneArchitect, a training-free framework that jointly improves structural and semantic consistency during progressive scene growth. Structural Consolidation integrates complementary far- and near-field monocular depth cues through foreground-aware alignment and fusion, reducing depth discontinuities and stabilizing geometry across views. Semantic Grounding maintains a global scene reference and performs target-aware prompt refinement, guiding local completion to remain consistent with the overall scene context. To further improve the final representation, we introduce a depth-regularized Gaussian refinement strategy with staged optimization, which enhances geometric stability while preserving fine-grained appearance. Extensive experiments on diverse indoor and outdoor scenes demonstrate that SceneArchitect consistently outperforms representative baselines in structural coherence, semantic consistency, and perceptual quality, while substantially reducing cross-view depth and reprojection errors.
